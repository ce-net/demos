Sixteen demos.

Things a network of ordinary devices can do together. Each one is its own open-source repo, built from reusable cells, with its recorded proof inside.

  • Works

    Print only through the guard

    Send a print job straight to the printer and it is refused. Send it through the guard, which checks bounds, temperatures, material and time: the 300 C job is rejected, the safe one prints, and a replay is refused.

    Fresh clone: 4 attempts, 3 refused with the rule that decided, 40 of 40 layers streamed; report matches byte for byte.
    Source and proof
  • Works

    Every call leaves a receipt

    Run 200 calls across four cells, download the receipts, and check them on any machine with no network. Edit one and the verifier names it; drop one and the chain shows the gap.

    Fresh clone: 200 of 200 receipts valid, byte for byte. The tampered and gapped files fail. CI checks offline.
    Source and proof
  • Works

    Shared with my devices, nobody else’s

    Enroll your laptop, phone and desktop with a pairing code; each gets a key bound to that device. A copied key file is refused, and a lost phone is revoked from the laptop while the other two keep working.

    Fresh clone: 3 devices accepted, the copied key refused by the holder proof, the revoked phone refused 81 ms after the revoke; 11 of 11 golden vectors pass ce-net-capability-verify.
    Source and proof
  • Works

    A factory line you can audit

    A three-station line runs as a twin and drill becomes the bottleneck. Download the signed event log: your browser checks every signature and recomputes the numbers. Edit one timestamp and it names the event that broke.

    Fresh clone: seed 42 reproduces the committed log byte for byte, 320 of 320 signatures verify, the tampered file fails on event 116 (drill).
    Open the page Source and proof
  • Works

    A cell that takes its data with it

    A notes cell moves from process A to process B while a client writes to it 20 times a second. B takes over under a higher epoch, A answers stale writes with “moved, epoch 2”, and writes sent, acknowledged and present all match.

    Fresh clone builds in under a minute; recorded 10 moves at 20 writes/s and 100 moves at 1600 writes/s with 0 writes lost or duplicated and the same hash-chain head on both sides. CI repeats the 100 moves on every push (median freeze 0.65 ms).
    Source and proof

  • Partly

    Sharded search: one leader per shard, rebalanced live

    4.6 million Wikipedia abstracts in 32 shards with 3 replicas each, one leader per shard. Kill a machine under a query loop that never stops: new leaders within one lease, replicas back to three, recall unchanged.

    Recorded run: 96 instances, 7 leaders lost with machine-c, new leaders within 3.65 s, all shards back to 3 replicas in 6.57 s, 0 failed queries, recall@10 0.9202 before and after. All machines are processes on one server; the ce-net crates it was designed around are replaced by public stand-ins.
